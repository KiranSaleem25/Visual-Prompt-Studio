Prompt A: Create a drawing of a large tree with spreading branches, thick roots, and green leaves. Use colored pencils on drawing paper, with a graphite pencil, eraser, and colored pencils. Apply layering to add depth and color. Use lines for branches, shapes for leaves, contrast between light and dark areas, emphasis on the trunk, and texture for the bark. Create balance and unity by arranging the branches naturally and blending colors smoothly.
Prompt B: Create a large tree with spreading branches, thick roots, and many leaves using woodblock printing. Use a woodblock, carving tools, a pencil, printing ink, a brayer, and paper. First, sketch the tree on the woodblock. Carefully carve around the branches, roots, and leaves using carving tools. Make deep cuts to shape the trunk and thick roots, and use fine, curved lines to show the branches, leaf veins, and bark details. Use lines for branches, shapes for leaves, contrast between dark ink and white paper, emphasis on the trunk, and texture on the bark. Finally, roll ink onto the woodblock with a brayer and press it onto paper to create the print.paper to create the print.
